Lluis Barba arrives at Barcelona Gallery Weekend 2020

Ninoska Huerta Gallery feels very proud to announce that our artist Lluis Barba is presenting his project “Old Threats. New armies” at Barcelona Gallery Weekend 2020

This project integrates photography, sculpture, and video in an interactive space. An experience that you can enjoy until September 20th at Contrast Gallery.

Barba is known for his understanding and reinterpretation of reality that he translates in Images that remind us of how absurd is our way to see the world we live in.
