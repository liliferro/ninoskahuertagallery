Exposición · 7 de septiembre de 2024

“Geometry in Monochrome” – Conversation

Dear friends,

I am pleased to invite you to an artists and curators’ conversation on Tuesday, July 30th, in conjunction with the current exhibition “Geometry in Monochrome” at MIFA, Doral.

The event will start at 6:30 pm with a cocktail, followed by the discussion at 7:00 pm.

Artists participants:

Adriana Dorta, Luis Gómez Rincón, Paul Amundarain, Valeria Yamamoto.

Mifa art Director Marco Caridad

Curator: Ninoska Huerta.
