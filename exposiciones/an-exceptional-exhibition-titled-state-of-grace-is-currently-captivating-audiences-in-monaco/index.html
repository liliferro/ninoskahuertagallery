Exposición · 6 de febrero de 2024

"An exceptional exhibition titled "State of Grace" is currently captivating audiences in Monaco.

Ver en YouTube ↗

Renowned visual artist Marcos Marin has curated a spectacular display comprising 25 monumental sculptures, showcased at the Jardins des Boulingrins et de la Petite Afrique, just in front of the iconic Casino de Monte Carlo. The exhibition, inaugurated on February 1, 2024, will be open for the next 2 months.

This unique showcase, which I had the privilege of being invited to its inauguration, pays tribute to Princess Grace of Monaco and her artist friends from various corners of the globe, who played a significant role in her life before her royal journey.

Noteworthy figures like Maria Callas and Josephine Baker, who once called Monaco home, are also honored in this exhibition. What truly stands out about this sculpture exhibition is its remarkable ability to capture the authentic essence of these artists, creating an ambiance that feels as if they are present within the exhibition itself.

Heartfelt congratulations to Marcos Marin for his exceptional creativity and to the organizers for orchestrating such a magnificent event. I’m confident that this exhibition will draw in a diverse audience, including prominent businessmen, Monaco’s Jet Set personalities, and a multitude of collectors and guests from around the world.

Ninoska Huerta.
